★ Pixel rules

No gradients. No anti-aliasing. No soft shadows. Every border is 4px solid, every drop shadow is a hard pixel offset (4/4 or 6/6). Use image-rendering:pixelated on art assets.

★ Contrast & readability

Press Start 2P is dense — never under 11px and always paired with VT323/Silkscreen for body. Neon on --bg-void hits AAA at 14px+.

Typography

Three fixed bitmap families. No weight axis — variation comes from size and color.

VT323 · Body

Terminal pixel face. Wide x-height, scales gracefully from 16→28px for body, descriptions, instructions.
